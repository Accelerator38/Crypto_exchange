# Forecast versus position, v1

Registered bounded offline research, 12 October 2026. No live execution, collector, exchange network, pytest, credentials or background processes. All nine safety flags remain false; local research is explicitly user requested, not runtime authority.

Step 2 reuses the exact frozen C predictions: 3-day gross horizon, daily labels and decisions, 3-day refit. C retains units while its forecast exceeds 20 bps. C_H enters strictly above 48 bps (20 bps plus 28 bps planned stress roundtrip) and exits at/below zero; holding has no fictitious new-entry fee. C_V retains the original C signal but rebalances target weights daily under the registered causal covariance budget. Costs are charged on actual delta units, including every sizing change.

Risk controller: 60 daily log returns ending one full day before the decision, 20% diagonal shrinkage, 20 bps/day per-asset volatility floor, 50 bps/day portfolio volatility budget, 10% per-asset and 80% gross decision-target caps, no shorting/leverage. These are research values, not approved real-money risk. Actual risk and exposure can deviate from forecasts; target caps are not continuous intraday guarantees.

EMA_V and Passive_V use the same risk controller, daily decision clock, costs and asset caps. EMA direction is feature 0 > 0; Passive direction is all eight assets on. Passive_V is a risk-controlled daily-rebalanced passive direction, not frictionless buy-and-hold. EMA_M/Passive_M use C_V's contemporaneously known requested gross, subject to their own identical risk caps and capacity. They are conditional allocation diagnostics, not model-free agents. Achieved exposure/risk ratios are always reported; matching is never inferred merely from common constraints.

Attribution is an exact path-conditioned accounting identity, not causal investment counterfactuals: baseline 80% equal-weight market presence, signal exposure timing, cross-asset signal selection, and actual position sizing/retention drift. Subtract actual fees/slippage and add signed funding to reconcile NAV change. The reference uses the actual path's previous NAV; its components are not independently investable returns. Episodes count complete zero-to-positive-to-zero holdings, not every rebalance fill. All ledgers start with 10000 and close terminally.

Step 4 uses calendar Q1/Q3 of 2024/2025 chosen before outcomes, separate continuous ledgers, 123 causal uniform ridge/scaler fits at 3-day origins, matured labels with seven bars purge plus one conservative availability bar. All mature origins enter the expanding training set; inner splits are hashed but not used for tuning. Older funding is missing, so normal/stress use explicit synthetic long carry of 1/3 bps per eight hours, never an implicit zero. Historical price and funding execution realism remain unqualified.

Four primary paired contrasts, evaluated on report stress and on stratified pooled-quarter stress. Primary bank block 7 days, audit role, shared 4096 draws. Eight registered contrasts use nominal Bonferroni percentile alpha .00625; 3/14 days and matched controls are secondary sensitivity. No cross-gap blocks, no claim that gaps were traded. Historical reuse and incomplete global attempt registry prevent an independent-validation or universal PBO/DSR claim.

Step 5 is gated: C_V must show absolute and incremental adjusted LCB > 0 against EMA_V and Passive_V on report and pooled regimes, stable leave-one-regime-out results and at least three positive incremental regimes; best regime share <=60%; actual report volatility/exposure ratios in [.8,1.25]. Otherwise no half-life tuning, MLP or GA. Only a passed gate would justify separately executing the registered uniform/90-day weighting pair with both gain and loss weights.

Step 6 prepares dependent-variance power sensitivity for 1/2/5/10 bps/day hypothetical true effects, one-sided alpha .025 per absolute/incremental hypothesis, power .8, at least 90 days, worst observed Bartlett/Newey-West variance across periods and lags 3/7/14. Zero true effect has no finite 80%-power test duration against zero. These calculations neither guarantee nonloss nor activate a prospective task. Freeze a rolling training recipe separately from each future model/scaler SHA; select genuinely new data only after the signal/policy gate and final contract approval.

Commands run one foreground stage at a time. New artifacts are exclusive-created. Compilation precedes pure checks, then registration precedes forecasts/replays/fits. A receipt pins all results, source, folds and resample files; parent evidence is read-only. The paper on backtest overfitting is methodological context, not a computed PBO for this project: https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf .
